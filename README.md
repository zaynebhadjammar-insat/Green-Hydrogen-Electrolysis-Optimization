# Green Hydrogen Production – Energy Optimization of an Electrolysis Cell

**Academic Project (PFA) | 2025–2026**

**Institution:** National Institute of Applied Sciences and Technology (INSAT), Tunisia

**Research Laboratory:** LaNSER – Laboratory of Nanomaterials and Systems for Renewable Energies

## Project Overview

This project investigates the energy performance of alkaline water electrolysis for green hydrogen production through a combined experimental and process simulation approach.

The study focuses on the influence of electrode materials on electrochemical performance, alongside the simulation of an alkaline electrolysis process using Aspen HYSYS.

## Objectives

- Evaluate the electrochemical performance of different electrode materials.
- Analyze hydrogen evolution (HER) and oxygen evolution (OER).
- Compare electrode behavior using current–voltage polarization curves.
- Model hydrogen production, gas separation and KOH recycling.
- Assess energy consumption and process efficiency.

## Methodology

**1. Experimental Investigation**

- Electrochemical testing in an alkaline electrolyte (KOH).
- Comparative study of seven electrode configurations, including metallic electrodes, polyaniline and manganese oxide-based materials.
- Analysis of current–voltage curves and electrode performance.
- Investigation of polarity reversal effects.

**2. Process Simulation – Aspen HYSYS**

- Modeling of an alkaline water electrolysis process.
- Simulation of hydrogen and oxygen separation.
- Integration of electrolyte recycling and cooling systems.
- Evaluation of hydrogen production, specific energy consumption and system efficiencies.
